Baking, Simplified.

These two recipes are the cornerstones of a home baker's repertoire. Both can be made in about 45 minutes using standard pantry ingredients and a home oven. No fuss, just chocolate.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chewy in the middle, crisp at the edges. A reliable classic that benefits from a quick chill.

Yield: 24 cookies Active: 15 min Total: 45 min Oven: 375°F / 190°C

Ingredients

  • Unsalted butter, browned or just softened
  • Light brown sugar (packed)
  • Granulated sugar
  • Large eggs
  • Vanilla extract
  • All-purpose flour
  • Baking soda
  • Fine salt
  • Semisweet chocolate chips or chopped chocolate
  • Flaky salt for finishing (optional)

Instructions

Baker's Tips

  • Chill the dough: Even an hour makes a difference in texture and depth of flavor.
  • Don't overbake: They will look underdone in the center when you pull them out; they set as they cool.
  • Storage: Keep them in an airtight container with a slice of white bread to keep them soft for days.

One-Layer Chocolate Cake

A deep, moist chocolate cake that comes together in one bowl. Perfect for a quick celebration.

Yield: 8-inch cake Active: 10 min Total: 45 min Oven: 350°F / 175°C

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder (Dutch-process preferred)
  • Granulated sugar
  • Baking soda
  • Fine salt
  • Neutral oil (canola or vegetable)
  • White vinegar
  • Vanilla extract
  • Warm water or brewed coffee

Simple Ganache

  • Semisweet chocolate, chopped
  • Heavy cream
  • Fine salt (optional)

Instructions

Baker's Tips

  • Room-temp ingredients: Use room-temperature eggs for a smoother, better-emulsified batter.
  • Toothpick test: Trust a toothpick over the timer; it should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter.
  • Use Coffee: Using brewed coffee instead of water won't make the cake taste like coffee, but it will make the chocolate taste much deeper.
  • Don't overmix: Whisk until the lumps are gone, then stop. Overmixing can make the cake tough.
  • Cool fully: If the cake is still warm when you pour the ganache, it will soak in or slide right off.
