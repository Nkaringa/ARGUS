Classic Chicken Lasagna

A hearty, satisfying twist on the traditional favorite, layered with tender chicken, savory marinara, and a creamy three-cheese blend.

Prep Time 25 min
Cook Time 45 min
Total Time 1 hr 10 min
Servings 8
Difficulty Intermediate

Good Effects

High Protein

Chicken breast supports muscle maintenance and provides long-lasting satiety.

Calcium-Rich

Ricotta and mozzarella contribute to bone health and structural integrity.

Lycopene Boost

Cooked tomato sauce is a rich source of the powerful antioxidant lycopene.

Comfort & Mood

Warm, shared meals support emotional wellbeing and social connection.

Ingredients

8

For the chicken & sauce

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 24 oz marinara or crushed tomatoes
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning

For the cheese layer

  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 large egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste

For garnish

  • Fresh basil or parsley, chopped

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  2. Season chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning. In a skillet over medium heat, cook chicken in olive oil until golden and cooked through (~6 min per side).
  3. Remove chicken from skillet, let it rest for 5 min, then dice or shred into bite-sized pieces.
  4. In the same skillet, sauté garlic for 1 min. Add marinara sauce and the chicken back in. Simmer for 5 min to meld flavors.
  5. In a medium bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, parmesan cheese, and a pinch of salt/pepper. Mix well until creamy.
  6. Cook lasagna noodles in a large pot of boiling salted water until al dente (usually 2 min less than package directions). Drain and lay flat.
  7. Assembly: Spread a thin layer of sauce in the bottom of the baking dish. Lay 3-4 noodles over the sauce.
  8. Spread 1/3 of the ricotta mixture over the noodles, then 1/3 of the chicken sauce, and sprinkle with 1/2 cup mozzarella.
  9. Repeat layers twice more (noodles, ricotta, sauce, mozzarella). The final layer should be noodles topped with the remaining sauce and mozzarella.
  10. Cover with aluminum foil (tent it so it doesn't stick to the cheese) and bake for 30 minutes.
  11. Remove foil and bake for another 15 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
  12. Let the lasagna rest for 10-15 minutes before slicing. This helps the layers set. Garnish with fresh herbs and enjoy!

Tips & Variations

  • Save Time: Use a store-bought rotisserie chicken. Simply shred and skip the chicken cooking steps.
  • Go Veggie: Add a layer of sautéed spinach or mushrooms for extra nutrients and texture.
  • Make Ahead: Assemble the lasagna up to 24 hours in advance, refrigerate, and bake when ready (add 10 min to covered bake time).
  • Gluten-Free: Swap standard noodles for your favorite gluten-free lasagna noodles.
